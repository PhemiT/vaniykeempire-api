## 4. User Account, Library & Cart Guidance

### Account Management

<!-- link: /login -->

- **Registration & Login:** Users can log in or sign up via the header `Login` / `Sign In` buttons.
- **Dashboard:** Logged-in users can view their profile dashboard by clicking their name/avatar at the top right (e.g., `bukunmi's Dashboard`).
- **Sign Out:** Located inside the user menu and page footers.

### Accessing Content (My Library)

<!-- link: /library -->

- Purchased content (ebooks, audio, free additions) does not require repeat payments.
- Users can access their digital library at any time by navigating to **My Library** (`/library`).
- Free items (e.g., _Grind_, _Up For You_, _Egwu_) can be claimed instantly by clicking **"Add to Library"**.

### Shopping Cart

- Items added to the cart appear in the sliding **Cart Drawer**.
- Users can review items, remove content, or click **Checkout** to proceed with payment processing.

## 5. Payments, Guarantees & Refunds

### Money-Back Guarantee

- **Platform Promise:** _"If you don't get value for your money, we guarantee a full refund."_

### Payment Processing

- **Currencies & Rates:** USD ($) is the standard store display currency.
- **Accepted Payment Methods:**
  - **Stripe:** Debit and credit card payments.
  - **PayPal:** Pay with a PayPal account or linked funding source.
  - **Korapay:** For Nigerian Naira payments.
- **Payment Flow:**
  1. Selecting **Buy Now** or checking out from the **Cart** opens a secure checkout.
  2. Choose a payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Korapay) and authorize the payment.
  3. Upon completion, purchased items instantly transfer to the user's **My Library**.

### Refunds Policy Summary

- **Eligibility:** Completed purchases are eligible for full refunds if value expectations are unmet.
- **Processing:** Refunds are issued via payment administration to the original payment method. Card processing typically takes **5–10 business days** to reflect.
